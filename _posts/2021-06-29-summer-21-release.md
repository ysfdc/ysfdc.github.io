This post is going out a little late, as by the time you are reading it, [Summer '21](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.salesforce_release_notes.htm&type=5&release=232) has already hit the shelves. However, I still want to call out what I found interesting from this newest release.

- MFA is coming whether we want it or not - make sure to read through this [future release statement](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_general_mfa_requirement.htm&type=5&release=232).
- Lightning Usage app is getting another feature - [Active Licenses](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_license_metrics.htm&type=5&release=232) - nifty!
- [Topics](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_general_topics_for_objects_LEX.htm&type=5&release=232) are coming to Lightning, previously only Classic could users tag Topics to records, now they can in Lightning too!
- A re-release of being able to add [Custom Actions](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_lex_mru_list_actions.htm&type=5&release=232) to the Recently Viewed List View! Hopefully, this time it isn't too good to be true.
- If you have MyTrailhead, you can now add [due dates](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_general_ial_duedates_learninghome.htm&type=5&release=232) to the items in the learning path, helping users stay on track with what needs to be done when.
- Also, now available for MyTrailhead is the ability to add things like [custom videos](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_general_ial_customlinks.htm&type=5&release=232) and other external links to provide more materials to your users.
- I am super stoked about this, we all now get [three free](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_general_iag_freemium.htm&type=5&release=232) custom walkthroughs for In-App Guidance!
- A new [Prompt type](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_general_iag_targeted_prompts.htm&type=5&release=232) has been introduced, using specific targets on the page - still in Beta for Summer '21.
- Thankfully, an update that allows users to have less permissions, this one allows the folks who need to update/create [In-App Guidance](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_general_iag_permissions.htm&type=5&release=232) to do without Modify Metadata permissions.
- Another update to Lightning that previously was only in Classic, the [ability to see why](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_forcecom_sharing_view_record_access_lex.htm&type=5&release=232) a User has access to a record.
- A new forming of sharing rules is coming, [Restriction Rules](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_forcecom_sharing_restriction_rules_beta.htm&type=5&release=232), which are designed to lay on top of what already exists, adding more layers of protection - still in Beta for Summer '21.
- Finally, the ability to [set default values](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_forcecom_default_value_dependent_picklist.htm&type=5&release=232) for dependent pick-lists.
- If you use split mode a lot, you will like this update - the ability to have [Mass Actions available](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_forcecom_mass_actions_in_split_view.htm&type=5&release=232) right from the Split View, no new tab or window required.
- For those Experience Users out there, you will like this update - the ability to brand and customize the [Service Not Available Page.](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_networks_sna_page.htm&type=5&release=232)
- Another Experience addition, the ability to [redirect users](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_experiences_url_redirects.htm&type=5&release=232) to an Experience page from a non-Salesforce URL.
- [Build Your Own (LWR)](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_experiences_lwr_available.htm&type=5&release=232) is now available to everyone, no CMS license purchase required.
- Supposedly we are getting [performance updates](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_revenue_cpq_release_update.htm&type=5&release=232) to CPQ but it is going to have impacts potentially on how things look and feel - expect a post on this in the near future.
- [Case Email notifications](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_case_email_notification_owa.htm&type=5&release=232) can finally come from someone that isn't noreply@salesforce.com
- Honestly, this last one makes me sad, and I hope Salesforce decides to cancel it - they are [removing the Ref ID](https://help.salesforce.com/articleView?id=release-notes.rn_case_disable_refid_threading.htm&type=5&release=232) for email threading and now only referencing something in the email header - good luck to us all!

That concludes my findings for this release! Leave your favorites in the comments.
