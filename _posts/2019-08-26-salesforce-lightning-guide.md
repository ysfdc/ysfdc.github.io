The Salesforce Lightning Reference Guide has been designed to help Admins and users make the move to Lightning. In conjunction with all of the wonderful tools provided by Salesforce, this guide provides tips and inspiration to enable you to successfully cross the Lightning Transition finish line.

![LightningBookMockup](/images/eaf2c-lightningbookmockup-e1566681838376.png)

 

In this reference guide you will find information on the following topics:

- Getting Ready for Lightning
- Important Lightning Features
- Things to Keep in Mind when transitioning to Lightning
- Training and Adoption
