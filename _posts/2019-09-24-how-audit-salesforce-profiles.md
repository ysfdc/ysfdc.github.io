Recently, I wrote a series of posts called The Ultimate Profile Auditing Guide. The purpose of the guide is to help you navigate viewing Profiles on a large scale through Excel as a means of running an audit on your Profiles. Below is the list of posts in the order that they go in.

[The Ultimate Profile Auditing Guide – Intro](http://sf9to5.wordpress.com/2019/03/19/salesforce-profile-auditing-guide-1/)

This post guides you through the initial thought process behind the series and some uses cases of why auditing profiles may be important.

[The Ultimate Profile Auditing Guide – Setting up Security](http://sf9to5.wordpress.com/2019/04/02/profile-auditing-guide-setting-security/)

This post is designed for those who may be less familiar with all of the different ways that Salesforce handles security on a record and user basis.

[The Ultimate Profile Auditing Guide: Excel Pivot Tables](http://sf9to5.wordpress.com/2019/04/30/profile-auditing-guide-excel-pivot-tables/)

This post walks through how you set up Pivot tables in Excel, as not everyone is familiar with the process.

[The Ultimate Profile Auditing Guide: SOQL Statements](http://sf9to5.wordpress.com/2019/05/14/profile-auditing-soql/)

This post contains all of the best goodies. It has the SOQL statements that can be modified to get exactly the data you are looking for out of Salesforce.

[The Ultimate Profile Auditing Guide: Putting it all together](http://sf9to5.wordpress.com/2019/07/09/profile-auditing-putting-it-all-together/)

Lastly, this post takes the above posts and puts of the pieces together so you can see the process end to end.
