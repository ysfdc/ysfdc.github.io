I wanted to put this post out there ahead of the last series of posts that will go live on the site for the rest of the year. I have had a great time running SF9to5 over these last ten years. As of last year, I officially stepped away from the Salesforce ecosystem to work on other ventures. As a result, SF9to5 will enter a maintenance phase.

The site will continue to live on, at least for now, as long as there is still traffic. The CPQ-related materials available for purchase and download will remain available, though there will be no further updates to them (which coincides with Salesforce sunsetting the sale of CPQ, as it was anyway). Any questions or comments, I will still respond to.

There will be a single series of posts left that will go live the rest of the year, and then following that, there will be no more new content.

I want to thank everyone who has visited or been involved with the site over the years. It has been amazing to build this brand from nothing and be recognized for the value the CPQ material has brought to others.

Best SF9to5
